About Us

Our Mission

At OUTSMART, we believe every child deserves access to quality education that sparks curiosity and builds confidence. Our mission is to provide engaging after-school lessons that complement classroom learning and help students reach their full potential.

Our Story

Founded in 2025, OutSmart began with a simple idea: make learning fun and accessible for all students. What started as a small tutoring service has grown into a comprehensive platform offering lessons in Math, Science, Music, Art, and more. Today, we're proud to serve hundreds of families across Mauritius.

Our Team

Our team consists of certified teachers, passionate educators, and dedicated professionals who share a common goal: to inspire the next generation of learners. Every instructor undergoes thorough training to ensure they deliver the highest quality education.
